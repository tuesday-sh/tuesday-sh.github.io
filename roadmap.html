Public Roadmap

We keep our paywalls strictly tied to features that work today—no "coming soon" buttons inside the app. Here is what's live in v1 and what we're building next at SURF Incubator in Seattle.

Shipping in v1 LIVE

Progressive Hardware Discovery

Add devices with AI photo scans (15 free, unlimited on Pro), barcode/QR scanning, Gold Clues, or zero-friction rough entries that refine over time.

Matched Security & Lifecycle Bulletins

Plain-English CISA KEV, CVE, firmware update, CPSC safety recall, and End-of-Life alerts filtered to your tracked inventory.

128-Device Matrix & Multi-Site

Track up to 15 devices on 1 primary site for free, or unlock up to 128 devices per site across unlimited locations with Founding Member Pro.

Dossier Exports, EOL Forecast & Ask Claude

Free sovereign JSON backups for everyone, plus Pro printable PDF/CSV/Markdown dossiers, multi-year EOL forecasts, audit logs, and Ask Claude briefs.

Next for Pro Tier 1 IN DEVELOPMENT

Live Cross-Device Cloud Sync

Optional encrypted sync across your own phone, tablet, and desktop—included automatically for Founding Member Pro subscribers when it lands.

SureThingNet Home & Family Sharing

A dedicated companion app (SureThingNet Home) designed for non-technical family members, paired with shared site invites from Tuesday.sh Pro.

Integrations & Fleet (Tier 2) PLANNED

Home Assistant (HACS) Local Bridge

Optional LAN-only bridge to pull live device and firmware metadata from Home Assistant without exposing your network to the cloud.

Fleet: Team RBAC & Attestation Exports

Future tier for IT consultants and MSPs: multi-admin client delegation, automated remote telemetry agents, >128 devices/site, and signed Packablock inventory attestation reports.
