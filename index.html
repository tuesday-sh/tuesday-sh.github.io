Progressive discovery, not spreadsheet homework.

Most people give up on network inventories because they feel like they need every serial number on day one. Tuesday.sh is designed to let you add rough entries fast and sharpen them during normal seasonal check-ups.

01 / Capture

Add what you know today

Snap a photo of a router label, scan a barcode, pick a brand and device type, or save a rough guess like "Garage Smart Plug." Everything slots cleanly into the C1–C8 hardware spine.

02 / Filter

Only alerts that match your gear

Instead of doom-scrolling security feeds, Tuesday.sh matches CISA KEV vulnerabilities, manufacturer firmware releases, CPSC safety recalls, and End-of-Life notices directly against your inventory.

03 / Maintain

Run calm seasonal check-ups

Four simple rhythms (Hardware Lifecycle, Monthly Patch Tuesday, Seasonal Check-Up, Yearly Gear Check-In) nudge you to patch, confirm rough guesses, and retire dead hardware without alert fatigue.

Local-first data. Zero photo retention.

Your home network map is sensitive. We built Tuesday.sh so you own your inventory file and can verify how camera frames are handled.

Ephemeral RAM-only camera vision

When you use optional AI photo identification to read a label or identify a chassis, frames travel over TLS 1.3 with X-Tuesday-Image-Retention: none_ephemeral_ram_only.

We analyze the image in volatile memory and wipe it immediately. We never write user photos to disk, databases, logs, or cloud buckets.

Sovereign JSON backup, always free

Your inventory lives on your device—no mandatory account wall. Export or restore your entire network as a plain, readable JSON file to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or local storage anytime.

Our mobile client repositories are public so anyone can independently audit local discovery and privacy boundaries.
