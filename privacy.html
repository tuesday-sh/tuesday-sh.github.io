Tuesday.sh Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 • Operated by Packablock LLC (Seattle, WA)

Tuesday.sh (sh.tuesday.pro, sh.tuesday.app, and https://tuesday.sh) provides homelab, network hardware, and smart-home software update & security bulletin tracking. We design our mobile and web applications around strict data minimization and ephemeral processing.

1. Zero Camera Photo Storage (Ephemeral RAM-Only Processing)

When you use the optional AI photo hardware identification feature to identify a router, switch, or IoT device:

  • Camera frames are transmitted over encrypted TLS 1.3 with the strict header X-Tuesday-Image-Retention: none_ephemeral_ram_only.
  • Images are analyzed strictly in volatile memory (RAM) to extract hardware model numbers, barcodes, or MAC OUI prefixes and are immediately wiped from memory upon completion of the request.
  • We never write, store, or retain user camera photos on disk, in databases, in application logs, or in cloud object storage buckets.

2. Information We Collect

  • App-Scoped Device Identifier (X-Tuesday-Device-ID): Used solely for API rate limiting, abuse prevention, and associating your tracked hardware inventory with your session. Never used for cross-app tracking or advertising.
  • Tracked Hardware Model & Firmware Metadata: Vendor names, model numbers (e.g., UDM-Pro), Package URLs (PURLs), and labels you choose to track so we can match CISA KEV, CPSC Safety Recall, and End-of-Life (EOL) bulletins to your inventory.
  • Subscription Receipt Tokens: StoreKit 2 / Google Play Billing verification tokens used strictly to validate active Pro Tier 1 entitlements.

3. No Third-Party Ad Tracking or Data Selling

Tuesday.sh contains zero third-party advertising SDKs. We never sell, rent, or share your personal information or network inventory with data brokers or advertisers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

You can remove any tracked device or reset your site inventory directly inside the Tuesday.sh mobile app at any time. To request complete deletion of your associated device identifier records, email privacy@tuesday.sh or support@packablock.com.
