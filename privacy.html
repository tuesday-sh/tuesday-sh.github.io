Tuesday.sh Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 • Operated by Packablock LLC

Tuesday.sh (sh.tuesday.app and https://tuesday.sh) provides homelab, network hardware, and smart-home software update & security bulletin tracking. We design our mobile and web applications around strict data minimization and ephemeral processing.

1. Zero Camera Photo Storage (Ephemeral RAM-Only Processing)

When you use the optional Plug & Tag camera discovery feature to identify a router, switch, or IoT device:

  • Camera frames are transmitted over encrypted TLS 1.3 with the strict header X-Tuesday-Image-Retention: none_ephemeral_ram_only.
  • Images are analyzed strictly in volatile memory (RAM) to extract hardware model numbers, barcodes, or MAC OUI prefixes and are immediately wiped from memory upon completion of the request.
  • We never write, store, or retain user camera photos on disk, in databases, in application logs, or in cloud object storage buckets.

2. Information We Collect

  • App-Scoped Device Identifier (X-Tuesday-Device-ID): Used solely for API rate limiting, abuse prevention, and associating your tracked hardware inventory with your session. Never used for cross-app tracking or advertising.
  • Tracked Hardware Model & Firmware Metadata: Vendor names, model numbers (e.g., UDM-Pro), Package URLs (PURLs), and port labels you choose to track so we can match CISA KEV, CPSC Safety Recall, and End-of-Life (EOL) bulletins to your inventory.
  • Optional Account Email: If you create an optional Tuesday.sh account, your email address is used strictly for authentication and requested security digest notifications.

3. No Third-Party Ad Tracking or Data Selling

Tuesday.sh contains zero third-party advertising SDKs. We never sell, rent, or share your personal information or network inventory with data brokers or advertisers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

You can remove any tracked device or reset your site inventory directly inside the Tuesday.sh mobile or web app at any time. To request complete deletion of your account or associated device identifier records, email privacy@tuesday.sh or support@packablock.com.
